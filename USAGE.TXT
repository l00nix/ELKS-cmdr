Cmdr 0.4.0-beta3
cmdr [left-directory [right-directory]]
Tab switches panels; arrows select; Enter opens a directory.
1 help, 2 path, 3 view, 4 vi, 5 copy, 6 move, 7 mkdir, 8 delete,
9 shell, 0 quit. F1-F9 match 1-9; F10 quits. Letter shortcuts remain.
R refreshes listings and terminal size. Bare Esc cancels a prompt.
Copy/move refuse overwrites. Delete only files or empty directories.
ANSI terminal, including Telnet; 80x25 minimum, up to 250x100.
Size detection uses ioctl or ANSI DSR; 80x25 fallback. No recursive operations.
Panels grow with available memory; INCOMPLETE LIST warns of omitted entries.
